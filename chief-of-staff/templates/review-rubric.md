# Review rubric

The competency framework your company actually scores you against, in its own words.

`review --half` maps your ledger onto these rows. That mapping is the entire value: reviewers score
against the rubric in front of them, and strong evidence filed under a heading they aren't reading
counts as no evidence. Two people with identical years get different outcomes on this alone.

## How to fill this in

Copy the categories out of your HR system — SuccessFactors, Workday, Lattice, a PDF from your
manager, whatever it is. **Use their wording, not a tidier version of it.** Rewriting "demonstrates
technical judgment across ambiguous problems" as "good judgment" throws away the two words that tell
you what to collect evidence for.

Do it once per framework change, which is usually once a year at most.

If your company publishes level definitions (what Senior looks like vs. Staff), paste the row for
the level you're *targeting*, not the one you hold. That's the bar the case has to clear.

before** the window, while an empty rubric row is still something you can go and fill.
