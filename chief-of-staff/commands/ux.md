# UX

`/chief-of-staff:ux [question | ticket | --plan]`

Load [design-advisor](../skills/design-advisor/SKILL.md).

> Placeholders are roles, not products — see [CONNECTORS.md](../CONNECTORS.md).

## Run

**With a question or ticket** — answer that. Gather the constraints from `memory/` first; a
recommendation that ignores the design system or proposes rebuilding something already in handoff is
worse than none.

**With nothing** — read the *Open questions* across `memory/projects/*.md` and ask which one. Don't
pick one silently, and don't answer in general.

**`--plan`** — the sequencing half. Ticket states from `~~tracker`, what's blocked and on whom,
what's in handoff, what has a date. One priority for the week, not five, and say what to drop.

## Depth

Use the host's design skills — critique, accessibility, interaction patterns, IA, UX writing — for
craft depth rather than restating them. Say which one you leaned on.

## Bars

- Cite or don't claim. Never invent a research finding, a statistic, or a source.
- Options with tradeoffs, not one confident answer.
- If the pattern isn't settled, say so and reason from the nearest solved problem instead.
- Anything worth keeping goes to `reviews/trends.md` with its date and the question it answered.
