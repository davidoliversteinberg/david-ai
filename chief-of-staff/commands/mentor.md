# Mentor

`/chief-of-staff:mentor [--arc <name>] [--opportunity <thing>] [--packet]`

Load [career-mentor](../skills/career-mentor/SKILL.md).

> Placeholders are roles, not products — see [CONNECTORS.md](../CONNECTORS.md).

## Run

**Default** — read `ledger/projects.md`, the ledger files since the last run, and
`memory/context/goals.md`. Report: where the case stands arc by arc, thin spots, legibility gaps,
one or two moves. Cite the ledger on every claim.

**`--arc <name>`** — one arc in depth. What would turn it from a project into a big win, who can
currently describe it, and what's missing.

**`--opportunity <thing>`** — should I take this? Answer against the current arcs, capacity, and
whether the work would be legible. **Declining is a valid answer** and should be given when it's the
right one.

**`--packet`** — hand off to [impact-ledger](../skills/impact-ledger/SKILL.md) for the roll-up. This
command assesses; that one drafts.

## Notes

- Reads **work origin only**.
- Nothing here reads HR or performance systems, by policy — see *What this skill will not do*.
- No outcome predictions. It reports what the evidence supports.
- A month with no progress reports as a month with no progress, not as encouragement.
